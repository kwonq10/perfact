2. Subscription Plans

The only paid plan currently available is Web Pro (billed monthly).

With Web Pro you can:

  • run free-time searches in the web app without a usage limit
  • adjust the position of your background image
  • customize the white display area of the card

On the free plan you can run up to 3 searches per week, counted across the web app and the Chrome extension combined. Unused searches do not carry over to the following week. The count resets based on your own time zone.

"Extension Pro" and "All Pro" are being prepared. They are not on sale and cannot be subscribed to at this time. If we begin offering them, we will announce the applicable conditions separately.

You can hold one paid plan at a time.

If you return to the free plan, the background position and white-area display revert to the standard settings, but the values you used on Pro are kept rather than deleted. If you subscribe again, we restore your previous settings.

5. Subscription Period and Automatic Renewal

Billing is monthly only. We do not offer annual billing.

There is no free trial.

The subscription period is one month, starting on your own subscription start date. Unless you cancel, it renews automatically at the end of the period and the same amount is charged (or the amount after the move described in Section 3, if that applies to you).

When Pro features become active

  • Pro features become active after we have confirmed that your first payment completed
  • Returning to Sukima from the payment screen does not by itself activate them. There may be a short wait until confirmation from the payment processor arrives
  • If the first payment does not complete, Pro features are not granted

6. Plan Changes

If you move to a higher plan, the new plan becomes active at the time of the change and the difference is prorated over the remaining period.

If you move to a lower plan, the new plan applies from your next renewal date, after the current period ends. Until then you keep your current plan.

Because Web Pro is currently the only paid plan available, plan changes will become usable after we begin offering other paid plans.

The currency cannot be changed while a subscription is active, as described in Section 3.

7. Payment Failures

If a payment cannot be confirmed, for example at renewal, the payment processor may retry the charge within a certain period.

During that time we operate the service so that Pro features may continue for up to seven days. If payment still cannot be confirmed after seven days, the account returns to the free plan.

This grace period is an operational practice intended to give you time to update your payment method. It is not guaranteed as a right, and we may change its length or how it is handled without prior notice.

During the grace period we show a notice asking you to update your payment method. The payment processor may also send you payment-related notifications.

As described in Section 5, if your first payment does not complete, Pro features are not granted and this grace period does not apply.

8. Cancellation

You can request cancellation at any time from the billing portal.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current subscription period. Your access does not stop at the moment you make the request.

  • After you request cancellation, you keep Pro features until the current period ends

  • Before the period ends, you can undo the cancellation request from the billing portal
  • Once cancellation has taken effect, you are not charged again

Even if you cancel partway through a period, we do not provide a prorated refund for that period (Section 9).

After deletion you can register again with the same Google account. You start again on the free plan.
